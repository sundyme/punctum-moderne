# BRIEF — Punctum Moderne · "Five Materials"

- workflow: general-video
- flow: automation
- storyboard: yes (shared as a progress deliverable; user asked for full effort, no further questions)
- aspect: 1920x1080
- length: 48s master, 60fps, synthesized score
- destination: brand film for the Punctum Moderne font site / showcase screening

## Intent
Apple-grade product-film grammar (dark void, single key light, macro, shallow depth of field,
few words) applied to a typeface. One dot passes through five materials that share its shape,
in chronological order — ink drop (1788), music-box pin (1796), punched hole (1890), inkjet
droplet (1984), light / bokeh (2026) — joined by match cuts on a single dot, ending with a
rack focus from defocused light discs to the sharp Punctum Moderne wordmark.
Retro and modern at once: every material is old; the rendering is precise and new.

## Customizations
- WebGL2 shader rendering, pure function of time (physically-styled materials, DOF, volumetric light)
- Score generated from the picture: music-box pins pluck real notes; ink drops, card reader,
  piezo ticks and the focus resolve are all timed from exported events
- Tempo grid 90 bpm (bar = 2.667s); chapter cuts on bar lines

## Direction left behind
The typical type-film: dots flying in to assemble letters, axis sliders demoed on a specimen grid.
